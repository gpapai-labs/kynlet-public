# Founder Interview Invitation

External-facing founder interview invitation for Kynlet. Derivative publication document for pre-booking founder outreach — not a product demo or sales asset.

**Live download:** [kynlet.com/resources/founder-interview-invitation.pdf](https://kynlet.com/resources/founder-interview-invitation.pdf)

**Repository archive:** [KYNLET_FOUNDER_INTERVIEW_INVITATION.pdf](./KYNLET_FOUNDER_INTERVIEW_INVITATION.pdf)

## Purpose

This asset is sent **before an interview is booked**.

It is intended to:

- increase founder interview booking rates
- explain why Kynlet is speaking with founders
- explain what Kynlet is exploring
- clarify that this is not a sales call
- reduce skepticism
- reduce booking friction
- make the call-to-action simple: reply to the email

It is not:

- a post-booking interview pre-read
- a sales asset
- a product brochure
- an investor pitch
- a fundraising document
- a product demo

## Approved usage

- founder outreach attachment
- warm-introduction follow-up
- semi-warm founder outreach
- reply-based booking request
- pre-booking validation conversation invitation

## Usage caveats

Use only with stage-accurate framing:

- pre-seed
- no live product
- no customers
- no revenue
- no completed validation targets yet
- no design partners committed
- no investor commitments
- validation evidence collection beginning

Do not use this asset to imply:

- product-market fit
- traction
- customer validation
- willingness-to-pay validation
- design partner commitments
- investor commitments
- a working product
- a sales process
