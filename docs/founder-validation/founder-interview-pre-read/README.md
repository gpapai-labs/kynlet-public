# Founder Interview Pre-Read

External-facing founder interview pre-read for Kynlet. Derivative publication document for booked validation conversations — not a product demo or sales asset.

**Live download:** [kynlet.com/resources/founder-interview-pre-read.pdf](https://www.kynlet.com/resources/founder-interview-pre-read.pdf)

**Repository archive:** [KYNLET_FOUNDER_INTERVIEW_PRE_READ.pdf](./KYNLET_FOUNDER_INTERVIEW_PRE_READ.pdf)

## Purpose

This asset is sent **after an interview has already been booked**.

It is intended to:

- reduce interview no-shows
- build trust
- reduce preparation anxiety
- explain what Kynlet is
- explain what we are trying to learn
- clarify what happens during the interview
- make clear this is not a sales call

It is not:

- a sales asset
- a marketing brochure
- an investor pitch
- a fundraising document
- a product demo

## Approved usage

- post-booking founder interview pre-read
- technical founder interview preparation
- CTO interview preparation
- Wizard-of-Oz session pre-read when appropriate
- design-partner candidate pre-read after a conversation is scheduled

## Usage caveats

Use only with stage-accurate framing:

- pre-seed
- no live product
- no customers
- no revenue
- no completed validation targets yet
- no design partners committed
- no investor commitments
- validation evidence collection beginning

Do not use this asset to imply:

- product-market fit
- traction
- customer validation
- willingness-to-pay validation
- design partner commitments
- investor commitments
- a working product
- a sales process
