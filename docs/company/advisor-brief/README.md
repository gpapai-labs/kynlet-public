# Advisor Brief

External-facing advisor brief for Kynlet. Derivative publication document for advisor and expert conversations — not an investor pitch or sales deck.

**Live download:** [kynlet.com/resources/advisor-brief.pdf](https://www.kynlet.com/resources/advisor-brief.pdf)

**Repository archive:** [KYNLET_ADVISOR_BRIEF.pdf](./KYNLET_ADVISOR_BRIEF.pdf)

## Purpose

Used for:

- advisor conversations
- warm introductions
- expert reviews
- domain expert feedback
- design-partner discussions

It is not:

- an investor pitch deck
- a fundraising deck
- a sales deck

## Approved usage

- advisor conversations
- warm introductions
- expert reviews
- design-partner discussions (context sharing only)
- founder-introduction context

## Usage caveats

Use only with stage-accurate framing:

- pre-seed
- founder validation beginning
- no live product
- no customers
- no revenue
- no completed validation targets yet
- no design partners committed
- no investor commitments

Do not use to imply:

- product-market fit
- traction
- customer validation
- willingness-to-pay validation
- design partner commitments
- fundraising progress
- working product or integrations
